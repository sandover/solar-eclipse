# Color selection methodology

Our aim is a palette whose colors belong together in a distinctive and
comfortable way. It should enrich sustained reading and writing, with enough
character to delight without demanding attention.

Treat the palette as a whole. Backgrounds, body text, supporting surfaces,
and accents shape one another's appearance. A pleasing color in isolation
may weaken the composition. Choose colors for their relationships and their
roles, rather than assembling individually attractive swatches.

Propose complete themes. Consider every visible color, including application
defaults that may sit outside the named palette. Feedback about one element
calls for reviewing its relationship to the whole, not an isolated patch.
Colors may remain unchanged when they still serve the composition.

Take methodology inspiration from [Solarized](https://ethanschoonover.com/solarized/):
use perceptual color science to understand and shape relationships in
lightness, chroma, and hue, alongside careful visual judgment. Its particular choices are not a formula that can simply be transferred into another color domain.

Give the background and main text particular care. Their relationship sets
the atmosphere and carries most of the reading experience. Supporting colors
should grow from that foundation. Restraint means purposeful differences,
not the removal of color or personality.

Evaluate colors in the context where people will use them. Area, repetition,
typography, surrounding colors, and display conditions affect the result.
Measurements inform judgment; they do not prove harmony or comfort.

Treat user feedback as evidence about the experience of the whole palette.
Explore the relationships that might explain it rather than replacing one
color with an unrelated favorite. Preserve what feels successful while
remaining willing to reconsider the foundation. Distinguish a plausible
explanation from something the user has actually confirmed.

Before presenting a change, inspect the actual rendered result in context,
including long passages and repeated accents. Assess the composition, not just
whether the requested values appeared. When claiming fidelity to a reference,
name the specific implementation and distinguish its actual assignments from
our adaptations. Keep that reference stable while exploring our own designs.
